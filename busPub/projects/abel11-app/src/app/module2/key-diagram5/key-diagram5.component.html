Overview of the Model

  • The IS curve is derived from the curves depicting desired national saving and desired investment.
  • Higher levels of output (income) increase desired national saving and shift the saving curve to the right.
  • The real interest rate at equilibrium between saving and investment in the diagram on the left is plotted in the diagram on the right along with the level of output on the horizontal axis.

Goods Market Equilibrium

  • The IS curve is derived from the curves depicting desired national saving and desired investment.
  • Higher levels of output (income) increase desired national saving and shift the saving curve to the right.
  • The real interest rate at equilibrium between saving and investment in the diagram on the left is plotted in the diagram on the right along with the level of output on the horizontal axis.
  • Use the slider to find the goods market equilibrium world real interest rate.
