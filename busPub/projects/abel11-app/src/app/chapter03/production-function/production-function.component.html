Marginal Product of Capital

  • Click or tap points on the production function to see the tangent line at that point. The slope of the tangent line equals the marginal product of capital at that point.
  • Or use the slider to move the tangent line along the production function by changing the capital stock.
  • Note that as the quantity of capital increases, the slope of the tangent line decreases so the marginal product of capital decreases, illustrating diminishing marginal productivity of capital.

The Production Function Relating Output and Labor

  • Click or tap points on the production function to see the tangent line at that point. The slope of the tangent line equals the marginal product of labor at that point.
  • Or use the slider to move the tangent line along the production function by changing the quantity of labor.
  • Note that as the quantity of labor increases, the slope of the tangent line decreases so the marginal product of labor decreases, illustrating diminishing marginal productivity of labor.

Adverse Supply Shock

  • Move the slider to the left to simulate an adverse productivity shock that lowers the marginal productivity of labor (MPN).
  • For any level of labor, the amount of output than can be produced is now less than before.
  • The slope of the production function is lower at every level of employment.
