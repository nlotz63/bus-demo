Exchange rate determination

Any shock that shifts demand up and to the right also shifts the supply curve up and to the left; similarly, any shock that shifts demand down and to the left also shifts the supply curve down and to the right. That’s because supply and demand of currencies are really just two sides of the same coin! (Pun intended.)
