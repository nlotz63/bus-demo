The effects of government purchases in an open economy

  • A simulation showing, step-by-step the effect of an increase in government purchases in the home country.
    • IS curve shifts to the right,
    • In the classical model, the full employment line also shifts right
    • Higher income reduces net exports (NX),
    • Lower NX at home, implies higher NX in foreign,
    • shifting foreign's IS curve to the right
    • The LM curve in both countries shift up and to the left to restore long-run equilibrium.
