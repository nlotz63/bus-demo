The Multiplier Effect

  • Use this interactive to explore a variety of scenarios to help you better understand comparative advantage and the gains from trade.
  • The graphs show the opportunity cost of producing cherries for each person, or simply the slope of the PPF.
  • The opportunity cost of picking apples is the inverse of the opportunity cost of picking cherries.
  • Whoever has the lowest opportunity cost picking cherries has a comparative advantage in picking cherries. The other person has a comparative advantage in picking apples.
