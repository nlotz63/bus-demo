The Effects of Entry on Profit

Overview:

  • A monopolistic market is characterized by differentiated products and free entry and exit (no or low barriers to entry).
  • Positive economic profit will attract new firms to the market, which causes the firm’s demand curve to become flatter. This reduces the profit maximizing price and quantity.
  • Use the slider to see how entry affects the firm’s profits.

Results:

The firm is earning positve economic profit. Other firms will enter the market, but as they do, each firm will earn lower profits. The firm is earning zero economic profit. At this point, there is no incentive for additional firms to enter the market. The market is in long-run equilibrium. Firms are earning negative economic profit (a loss). Some firms will exit the market until the remaining firms are earning zero economic profit.
