The Quantity & Price Effects from a Price Decrease

Key Points:

  • Marginal revenue (MR), following a price decrease, can be decomposed into two parts: price effect (PE), and quantity effect (QE).
  • The decrease in price, lowers total revenue, while the increased quantity sold increases total revenue.
  • When the price change occurs in the elastic portion of the demand curve (prices above $3), the quantity effect dominates, resulting in marginal revenue being positive.
  • When the price change occurs in the inelastic portion of the demand curve (prices below $3), the price effect dominates, resulting in marginal revenue being negative.
