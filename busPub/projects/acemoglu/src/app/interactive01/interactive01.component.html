Equilibrium in the Supply/Demand Model

Equilibrium and disequilibrium

  • The competitive equilibrium price is the price at which quantity supplied (qs) and quantity demanded (qd) are the same (the instersection of the supply and demand curves). This is sometimes referred to as the market clearing price.
  • When the market price is above the competitive equilibrium price, quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded, creating excess supply.
  • When market price is below the competitive equilibrium price, quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied, creating excess demand.
