Marginal Revenue and Revenue

Instructions:

  • Use the slider to increase the quantity produced to discover four key quantities.
  • When a key quantity is attained, additional information will be shown in the message area.
  • Profit and revenue equations will be updated as you change the quantity.

Key equations and values:

Message area:

Marginal revenue equals marginal cost. At this quantity, profit is maximized.

Marginal revenue equals zero. At this quantity, revenue is maximized. This model shows that maximizing revenue is not the same as maximizing profit. Recall profit is maxizmed at a quantity of 600 million pills.

Price (demand curve) equals average total cost (ATC). At this quantity, profit equals zero (or normal economic profit).

Price (from the demand curve) equals marginal cost (MC). At this quantity, the monopolist is earning a negative profit.
