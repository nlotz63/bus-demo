PPC: Change in Technology

Key Points:

  • Changes in technology can favor one product over the other.
  • Use the first slider to allocate how much a change in technology benefits each product.
  • Then use the second slider to simulate an increase in technology.
  • Notice at the extremes, a change in technology has no effect on the other good.
  • When split 50-50, each good benefits equally and the PPC shifts parallel to the initial curve.
