Production Possibilities Frontier

Opportunity Cost

  • The amount of {{ graph().yTitle | lowercase }} that must be given up to produce another unit of {{ graph().xTitle | lowercase }} is the opportunity cost of producing {{ graph().xTitle | lowercase }}.
  • It is equal to the slope of the PPF.
  • For a straight line, the opporunity cost is constant, while for a bowed out PPF, the opportunity cost increases as more {{ graph().xTitle | lowercase }} are produced.
  • Mouseover points on the PPF to view the opportunity cost at that point.

Efficient production

  • All points along the PPF are efficient.
  • Points within the PPF are attainable but not efficient.
  • Points outside the PPF are unattainable.
